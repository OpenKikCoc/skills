# Evidence calibration checklist

Use this shared core before reporting conclusions, comparisons, contributions,
or quantified plans. It is a wording/scope check, not another lifecycle, ledger,
or universal confidence enum. Preserve each domain's stricter evidence schema.

1. **Bind every material claim.** Identify the source, exact subject, time or
   version, observation method, and scope. A cluster, candidate, source commit,
   team result, or proposed plan cannot stand in for another subject. Separate
   direct observations, extracted source facts, inference, and proposals.
2. **Match wording to evidence strength.** Use confirmed/caused/achieved only
   when the domain's discriminator and verification support them. A plausible
   mechanism, correlated timestamp, simulation, or historical precedent supports
   a bounded hypothesis, not a present causal conclusion. Say what the check
   proves and what it leaves untested; avoid broader coverage claims.
3. **Preserve attribution and disclosure boundaries.** Others' work and team
   outcomes are not the author's personal contribution. Private or personal
   material is not public evidence merely because the agent can read it. Name
   the actual source/owner and authorized audience; summarize or withhold
   sensitive details where required. Approval to read is not approval to publish.
4. **Keep unknowns explicit.** Do not fill gaps with a confident narrative.
   State the missing evidence, competing explanation, and discriminating next
   check. Label an unsupported estimate or future target as a hypothesis or
   proposal; do not quietly convert it into an observed result.
5. **Repair comparisons rather than erase them.** Align numerator/denominator,
   baseline, time window, version, population, attribution, and measurement
   method. Prefer a corrected, scoped comparison with its limitations over
   deleting inconvenient evidence. If no defensible comparison remains, state
   why and leave the unsupported delta unknown rather than inventing a number.
6. **Recheck the final wording.** Can a reader trace the claim to the evidence
   without assuming hidden context? Does the verb, quantity, ownership, and
   scope remain supported after edits? Preserve corrections and material
   counterevidence; a cleaner story is not a stronger result.

## Domain contracts keep their own rules

Domain skills own stricter evidence rules: `ops-k8s-rca` (conclusion rules) and
`hr-interview-feedback` (`references/evidence-calibration.md`) are examples.
Read the owning contract when working in that domain. Do not replace its
labels, numbers whitelist, source permissions, safety gates, or acceptance
criteria with this checklist, and do not treat a passing prose check as runtime
verification.
