# Kickoff checklist

Fill CONTRACT from the owner's words; anything unknown becomes an open question
with a safe default, not a drafting blocker.

1. Audience, purpose, planning horizon.
2. Ownership of the live document and the allowed write scope; ownership
   evidence type (metadata, or the owner's verbatim statement plus verified
   identity). Record which.
3. Standalone, or a contribution to a larger document (then declare "this
   section is a subset of <main doc> at <layer>" and link it).
4. Voice, language, terms to keep, names and terms to ban.
5. Sources, and whether private notes may be cited (default: no).
6. Number authority: who may set targets (default: nobody; the plan proposes).
7. Skeleton and focus. A skeleton for a new document or an owner-requested
   restructuring is settled before prose; an existing owner-iterated structure
   is accepted as is.
8. Figures and measurable style constraints (pronoun count, parentheses,
   arrows).
9. Review lanes, must-fix definition, who authorizes publication.
10. Downstream write approvals and conflict policy.

Stop only for non-defaultable decisions: publish target, approval over someone
else's document, scope additions. Initialization records decisions; it is not
approval.
